Sensors & Actuators

a. What's a sensor?

It is a device that detects physical, chemical, or environmental changes; such as heat, light, pressure, and motion, and converts them into an electrical signal.
It acts as the system's "senses", like eyes, ears, or skin, and is placed at the input port.

. Examples:

-Temeparture sensors(measure heat), light-dependent resistors(measure light levels), pressure sensors(measure force in gases or liquids), motion sensors(detect movement)

b. What's an actuator?

It is a device that receives an electrical control signal and converts it into a physical motion.
It acts as the system's "muscles", and is placed at the output port.

. Examples:

-Electric motors(produce rotational or linear motion), heaters(produce thermal output), buzzers & speakers(produce sound)
