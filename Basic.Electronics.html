|. What is Electronics?

Electronics is the control of tiny electrical particles called electrons to store, send and process information.
The difference between it and electricity is that basic electricity just moves a lot of raw energy to do heavy work, such as power lines for example, whereas electronics uses tiny amounts of electricity to send the signals.


||. Current, Voltage & Resistance

Current, voltage, and resistance are the three core and fundamental pillars of any electrical circuit. All linked by Ohm's law : V=I*R.



|||. Types of Connections

There are two types of connections that every beginner should know

a. Series Connection

Components are connected end-to-end along a single path, meaning the same electric current flows through every device. If one component breaks or is removed, the entire circuit stops working.
Current(I): Itotal=I1=I2=I3
Voltage(V): The total voltage supplied is divided among the individual components. Vtotal= V1+V2+V3
Resistance(R): The total resistance is the sum of all individual resistances. Rtotal= R1+R2+R3

b. Parallel Connection

Components are connected across the same voltage points with multiple branching paths. Each device operates independently; if one breaks or is removed, the others keep working!
Current(I):The total current is divided among the parallel branches. Itotal= I1+I2+I3
Voltage(V): Vtotal=V1=V2=V3
Resistance(R): The total resistance decreases as more branches are added. The reciprocal of the total resistance is the sum of the reciprocals of all individual resistances. 1 R total= 1 R 1+ 1 R 2+ 1 R 3
