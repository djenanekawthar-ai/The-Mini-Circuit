Electronic Components

These are the main, most used, & most important electronic components to use when building any circuit :

1. Resistor

A crucial element in every circuit. It limits current, creates voltage drop, and protects other components from electrical overload. Its ability to reduce the current is called resistance and is measured in Ohms.


2. LED

Stands for Light-Emitting Diode. It produces light when an elecric current passes through it. Allows current flow in one direction only.


3. Capacitor

It is a passive electronic components with two terminals that stores and releases electrical energy. It is used to stabilize circuits, filter signals, and manage power.

4. Diode

A component that allows current flow in one direction (just like a LED). It has low resistance in one direction, and high resistance in the other. It converts AC to DC in power supplies, which can protect other components.

5. Transistors

It is a tiny semiconductor device used to regulate voltage, control electronic signals, or amplify electrical power.

6. Integrated circuit (IC)

Commonly called a microchip, is a tiny semiconductor block that houses interconnected electronic components like transistors, resistors, and capacitors. It performs complex functions like amplification, and handles digital logic operations.


7. Potentiometer

Often called a variable resistor. It is a three-terminal passive electronic component that acts as a manually adjustable voltage divider.
