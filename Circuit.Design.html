Circuit Design

This is a list of symbols used in circuit designs (schematics) that represent electronic components.



. How to read a schematic?

Click here! to find a quick explaination of how circuit designs should be read.


. Components connected in series

Components are connected one after another, forming a single path for current.

Component Typical use
Resistors Limit current
LEDs Light indicators
Switches & buttons Control current flow
Batteries Connect voltage sources in series
Fuses Protect circuits
Diodes Control current direction


. Components connected in parallel

Components are connected across the same two points, creating multiple paths for current.

Component Typical use
Resistors Voltage division and current distribution
LEDs(each with its own resistor) Multiple indicator lights
Capacitors Increase total capacitance
Batteries Increase battery capacity
Motors Operate multiple motors independently
Lamps Independent lighting branches


NOTE :

These are typical circuit arrangements, not strict rules. The correct connection depends on the component's function and the circuit's design.
